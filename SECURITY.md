# Authenticity and Security

The authentic files are those listed in [SHA256SUMS](SHA256SUMS) and signed by the key in
[VERIFY.md](VERIFY.md).

Please report privately to elsevier2012@hotmail.com:

- copies of the work sold, or distributed with modifications;
- accounts, sites or repositories impersonating the author or presenting themselves as official;
- files with matching names but non-matching checksums.

Use the subject line "Fractured Adelic Geometry authenticity report".
