# Names and Identity

"Fractured Adelic Geometry" identifies this work and its author, Vincent Liu. This policy
explains how the names may be used so that readers can tell the authentic work and endorsed
specifications apart from independent work.

**You may, without asking:**

- refer to the work by name when citing, reviewing, teaching or discussing it;
- describe an independent project as "based on", "inspired by", or "implementing ideas from"
  *Fractured Adelic Geometry*, with a citation.

**Please ask first before:**

- using the names in the name of a product, project, organization, domain or social-media account;
- describing anything as "official", "endorsed", "certified" or "reference" with respect to Fractured Adelic
  Geometry, or a construction as "the" realization of its open obligations;
- using the cover art or visual identity of the work.
